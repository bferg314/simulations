Phase Chimes: listening check

Renders 20 seconds of chimes offline with the sim's own voices and scheduler, then measures them. Each scenario is centred on the realignment at t = T, where every pendulum strikes at once. Peaks must stay below −1 dBFS. "Click residue" is what is left above 14 kHz with the low-pass taken out and every partial below 11 kHz, so anything there is a discontinuity.
